## 1. YOUR ROLE

Act as an award-winning creative director, senior UI/UX designer, expert brand designer, and senior frontend engineer.

Your mission is to DESIGN AND IMPLEMENT a complete, visually exceptional, production-quality **ONE-PAGE LANDING WEBSITE** for a fictional local flower shop in Laguna, Philippines.

BUSINESS NAME: PETAL & KNOT

TAGLINE: Where Every Bloom Is Made with Love.

BUSINESS TYPE: Boutique Flower Shop & Handmade Floral Gift Studio.

LOCATION: Santa Cruz, Laguna, Philippines.

The business specializes in:
- Fresh flower bouquets
- Dried and preserved flowers
- Handmade crochet bouquets
- Handmade fuzzy-wire / pipe-cleaner flowers
- Personalized floral gifts
- Wedding flowers and bridal bouquets
- Graduation bouquets
- Birthday and anniversary flowers
- Valentine's Day and Mother's Day bouquets
- Event flower arrangements
- Custom floral styling

### CORE DIRECTIVE

Build a premium boutique florist website, not a generic template, corporate website, dashboard, or full e-commerce store.

The result must feel like a real, expertly designed Philippine floral boutique website with an intentional brand identity.

The design must balance two identities:

1. Elegant and naturally beautiful floral arrangements.
2. Playful, meaningful, handmade flower creations.

The site should attract people buying affordable gifts and people commissioning premium event bouquets.

Use realistic Filipino pricing, Philippine peso notation, locally relatable occasions, and warm, approachable copywriting.
